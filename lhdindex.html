Rulebook

What Is This "New Style"?

Based on the new 3 on 3, 3ICE format. Designed to simulate fast-paced, overtime hockey throughout the entire game with almost zero whistles and maximum speed!

01

Overtime All The Time (3-on-3 Full Time)

3 skaters plus a goalie for each team for the entire duration. No standard 5-on-5 lulls—pure open-ice skating and scoring opportunities.

02

No Powerplays – Jailbreak Penalty Shots

Infractions result in a jailbreak penalty shot. Defending players start from the blue line while the shooter starts from the center dot.
